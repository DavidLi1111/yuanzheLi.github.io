Hello, I am Yuanzhe! I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Arizona, advised by [Prof. Huanrui Yang](https://sites.google.com/view/huanrui-yang), and a member of the Reconfigurable Efficient Intelligence Group.

My research focuses on **efficient Large Language Models**, especially for **multi-step LLM agents**. I work on:

- **Model compression**: post-training quantization and dynamic mixed precision, structural pruning, and (on-policy) distillation for recovery
- **Efficient attention**: linear attention / linearization and dynamic sparse attention
- **Robustness to compression**: training models and agents that stay accurate after pruning and quantization

Previously, I worked on LLM-based code generation for low-resource programming languages and on representation learning for limit order books.

**I am looking for research internships for Summer 2027 in efficient ML / model compression / LLMs.** Feel free to reach out by [email](mailto:yuanzheli@arizona.edu).
